Why chips, not text

  • Recognition beats recall. A provider typing from memory guesses at customer vocabulary and usually guesses wrong.
  • Free tags cannot be aggregated or filtered — they arrive in a mix of Dhivehi and English with inconsistent spelling.
  • Free text stays available underneath for genuine gaps, so nothing is lost.
  • Same reasoning as §1f's review tags, which are also fixed per category for the same reason.

Home — the encryption claim comes out

This one is not a redraw, it is a correction of a false statement. Chat is admin-readable through a dispute or report by design. Advertising encryption is a promise the system does not keep, and the worst possible moment to discover that is mid-dispute — which is exactly when someone will read it back to you.

Now claims only what the system delivers: contact details are never exchanged, and the agreed price is locked at acceptance (§1h).

Two claims tightened

  • Encryption → non-disclosure. The real, defensible promise is that a provider never gets the customer's number — which is the whole point of §1c and a stronger claim than most competitors can make.
  • Transparent Pricing gained a specific: Round 15's locked agreement means the price genuinely cannot move without the other party accepting an amendment. That is worth saying plainly.
  • Same honesty rule the plan already applies to payment status — never “Payment Verified”, always “Provider confirmed receipt”.

Pricing, per Round 16

  • Five models: fixed, hourly, daily, range, quote.
  • priceUnit is separate — job, hour, day, session, visit. It is what lets the app render “MVR 500/session” when session is not a pricing model at all.
  • Model constrains booking mode. range and quote force request mode — say so inline, as shown, rather than rejecting the combination at publish after the provider has built the whole listing.
  • Service Packages deferred. Tiered pricing multiplies through slots, quotes, price adherence and the booking record. Shipping a control nobody can book against is worse than not shipping it.
